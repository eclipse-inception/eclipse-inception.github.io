Eclipse INCEpTION™ is an incubating project at the [Eclipse Foundation](https://www.eclipse.org),
led by Richard Eckart de Castilho. The project's committers and status are listed on the
[Eclipse INCEpTION project page](https://projects.eclipse.org/projects/technology.inception). To get in touch, see the [contact page]({{ site.url }}/contact/).
[Find out more about the move to Eclipse]({{ site.url }}/eclipse/).

## Origins

INCEpTION started as a project at the Ubiquitous Knowledge Processing (UKP) Lab at the Department of
Computer Science, Technische Universität Darmstadt.
